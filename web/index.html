How each answer is checked

Every number you see is guarded the same way, before it reaches you. Being matched against a real published study is a stronger claim on top of that, and only one of the three can make it.
1I / ʻOumuamua
Computed here, and matched against the published Project Lyra figures (Hein et al. 2019).
2I / Borisov
Computed the same way. No one has published a study to check it against, so HITS does not claim one.
3I / ATLAS
Computed the same way. No one has published a study to check it against, so HITS does not claim one.

Who wrote the answer

Written by Granite, then checked number by number against the solver before you saw it.
Written by the deterministic template. HITS serves it when Granite is unavailable, and also when the generated answer would be the worse one, thinner than the template or claiming something the solver did not. Still every number gated, just not the model's phrasing.

All figures are patched-conic and two-body. HITS does not model n-body dynamics or non-gravitational forces. A figure is a faithful record of what the solver computed, not a claim that it is right to the precision it is quoted at.
